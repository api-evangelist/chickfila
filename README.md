# Chick-fil-A (chickfila)
Chick-fil-A is an American fast-food restaurant chain specializing in chicken sandwiches and operating over 3,000 locations across the United States. Chick-fil-A operates the Chick-fil-A One tiered loyalty program, the Chick-fil-A mobile app for ordering and rewards, the catering platform, and eGift Cards.

## APIs

### Chick-fil-A Mobile App
Mobile ordering, pickup, drive-thru express, delivery, and Chick-fil-A One loyalty access.

### Chick-fil-A One
Tiered loyalty rewards program with Member, Silver, Red, and Signature tiers.

### Chick-fil-A Online Ordering
Web ordering for pickup, drive-thru, curbside, dine-in, and delivery.

### Chick-fil-A Catering
Group catering ordering for party trays, packaged meals, entrees, and sides.

### Chick-fil-A eGift Cards
Email-deliverable digital gift cards in $5 to $100 denominations.

### Chick-fil-A Restaurant Locator
Search for nearby locations with services and hours filters.
