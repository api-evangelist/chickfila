By Zack Smith , Jake Wasdin and Kevin Koleck Since we first shared the work Chick-fil-A is doing at the Edge in 2018 , we have come a long way. A couple of years ago, we wrote about some of our updates, including the state of the platform , how we built support for it internally , and how we do GitOps . Not only is the platform now present in all our stores, but it is increasingly essential for more areas of store operations, yielding more use cases and increasing the importance of the platform’s reliability.
